Privacy notice

RAFACTools Planner helps a squadron plan its monthly training programme. This page says plainly what it keeps about people. The people who run this site are responsible for it.

What is kept

  • Your account: your email address, the name you choose to show (optional), your role in the squadron, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password. The password itself is never stored.
  • Sign-in sessions: a random code per browser, the kind of browser (for example "Firefox on Linux") and when it signed in, for 30 days or until you log out.
  • Your squadron's plans: programmes, activities, instructor names and ranks, notes, closures and weekend events.
  • The requirements tracker: cadets' names and short notes. Only enter what your unit's rules allow.
  • A security log: when the site administrator looks at your squadron, and when an owner invites, changes or removes a member. Kept for a year; your owners can read it.

Who can see it

Only signed-in members of the same squadron, the people who run this site (who can read the database), and, to help with a reported problem, the site administrator, who sees a squadron read-only and is logged doing so.

What is not done

  • No analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.
  • Nothing is sent to anyone else. Email is only sent to you for confirming an account, invitations and password resets, and, if you choose to send one, a problem report.
  • Cookies are only the ones needed to keep you signed in and to protect forms.

Your choices

  • Log out of other places on the your details page.
  • A squadron's owner can delete the whole squadron and everything in it from the squadron settings. It is permanent.
  • To have your own account removed or your data corrected, ask your squadron's owner or the people who run the site.

More detail is in the Help pages under "Security and privacy" once you are signed in.
