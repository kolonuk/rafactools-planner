Bank holidays and reminders

Each new month gets a one-day closure for every bank holiday in it, from the government's list (checked about once a month). You can edit or delete any of them. Owners and editors are emailed once for a parade night that still has an empty Activity or Instructor box that many days ahead.

Public link and calendar

Publish a month from its Actions menu and anyone with this squadron's link can see it, without signing in: a calendar they can subscribe to, and the month's PDF. Only dates, uniform and activity titles are shared. The link works for one month and then stops, so make a new one when you need it. Making a new link stops the old one straight away.

The squadron's last owner can't be removed, demoted or made inactive — promote someone else first. Inactive members can't sign in or reset their password until you make them active again. Send temporary password emails the member a new password (you never see it): their old one stops working, they must choose their own at their next sign-in, and it expires after 7 days.

Moves between squadrons

A person with a personal email address can move their login to another squadron. They ask on Your details, or an owner asks for them from the Members list above (and they must agree). An owner of the squadron they join accepts and chooses their role. While a move waits, the person's account is switched off. Only the login moves: this squadron's programmes and history stay here.
