Your login can move to another squadron that is already on this site. Only the login moves: your password, two-step sign-in and name come with you, and nothing else. Programmes, history, requirement entries and the instructor list stay with {{if .User}}your current squadron{{else}}the squadron you leave{{end}}, and you will no longer see them.

  1. You type the number of the squadron you are joining.
  2. Your account is switched off straight away, so you are signed out and cannot sign in.
  3. An owner of that squadron is emailed. They accept, and choose your role (owner, editor or viewer), or decline.
  4. When they accept, your account is switched on in the new squadron. If they decline, or 14 days pass, it is switched back on here. You can cancel at any time with the link in the email you will get.
