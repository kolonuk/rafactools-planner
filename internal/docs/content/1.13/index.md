# RAFACTools Planner help

RAFACTools Planner helps a squadron plan its monthly training programme and
print it as a one-page PDF.

Use the tree on the left to move around. It has three branches; open the one
that matches what you are doing.

## Using the planner

For everyone with a login: owners, editors and viewers.

1. [Claim your squadron](getting-started) (the first person from a squadron does this once).
2. [Build a programme](programmes) for a month, then [export the PDF](pdf-export).

| Topic | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](getting-started) | Claiming a squadron and confirming your email |
| [Signing in](signing-in) | Logging in and out, passwords, sessions, changing your email |
| [Programmes](programmes) | Creating a month, the grid, closures, weekend notes, the requirements tracker, history |
| [Instructors](instructors) | The instructor list, and setting your own name |
| [PDF export](pdf-export) | What the PDF contains and its limits |
| [Security and privacy](security-and-privacy) | What is stored, for how long, and who can see it |
| [Report a problem](report-a-problem) | Reporting something that went wrong, screenshots, and what is sent |
| [Known limits](known-limits) | Things the planner cannot do |
| [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting) | What each message means and what to do |
