# Time off

The **Time off** list (the link in the top bar) shows when instructors and other staff are away. Everyone in the squadron can see it, including viewers. It is there so that whoever plans a month does not put someone on a night they cannot do.

## Adding time off

- **Everyone** with a login, viewers included, can add time off for anyone: enter the first and last day and, if you want, a note such as "course". The name starts as your own; type another, or pick one from the suggestions (the instructor list and the members). Use the name the way it appears in the grid's Instructor boxes.
- The last day must be on or after the first, and an entry can cover at most 366 days. A squadron can hold at most 1,000 entries, so delete old ones; the list only shows time off that has not finished yet.

## Changing it

Click a date or note to edit it in place (tick to save, cross to cancel), or choose **Delete**. Everyone can change or delete any entry. To give an entry to someone else, delete it and add it again.

## The warning in the grid

In a programme's grid, an **Instructor** box that names someone who is away that night turns **red**, with a line under it such as "Fg Off Smith is away 3 Nov to 10 Nov (course)". It is a warning only: nothing is blocked and you can still save.

A name matches when it appears in the box as whole words, ignoring capitals and punctuation, so "Smith" matches `Fg Off Smith, Flt Lt Jones` but not `Smithson`. A person in the [instructor list](instructors) also matches by name without their rank.

## What is never shared

Time off is **never** in the PDF, the PNG or the [calendar feed](public-sharing), and it is only shown to people signed in to your squadron.
