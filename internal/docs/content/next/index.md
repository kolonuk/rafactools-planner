# RAFACTools Planner help

RAFACTools Planner helps a squadron plan its monthly training programme and
print it as a one-page PDF.

## If you are new

1. [Claim your squadron](getting-started) (the first person from a squadron does this once).
2. [Invite your staff](roles-and-invites) and choose what each person is allowed to do.
3. [Build a programme](programmes) for a month, then [export the PDF](pdf-export).
