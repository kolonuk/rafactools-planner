# Roles and invites

Every person has one role in their squadron.

| | Viewer | Editor | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the dashboard and programmes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Export a PDF | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create and edit programmes (grid, closures, weekend notes, requirements tracker) | No | Yes | Yes |
| Open [squadron settings](squadron-settings) | No | No | Yes |
| Invite people and revoke invites | No | No | Yes |

Viewers see programmes without the editing forms. A person only ever sees
their own squadron's information.

## Inviting someone

Only an **owner** can invite.

1. Open [Squadron settings](app:/squadron).
2. Under **Invite someone**, enter their email address and choose a role
   (**Editor**, **Viewer** or **Owner**).
3. Select **Send invite**.

They receive an email with a link. The link works **once** and expires after
**7 days**. They choose a password (12 to 72 characters) and are signed in
straight away. They do not need a RAFAC address and do not need to confirm
their email separately: opening the invite link is the confirmation. The
inviting owner is vouching for them, so only invite people you trust, and
think before choosing **Owner**.

An address can have only one account. Inviting an address that already has an
account fails when the invite is accepted; nothing about the existing account
changes.

## Pending invites

Squadron settings lists invites that have not been used or expired. **Revoke**
cancels one; the link stops working at once.

## Changing or removing people

There is currently no screen to change someone's role or remove them. See
[known limits](known-limits).
