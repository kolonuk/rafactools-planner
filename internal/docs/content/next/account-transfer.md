# Moving your login to another squadron

A login can move to another squadron that is already on this site. It is for people whose email address is **personal** (not the squadron-claim addresses of the form `oc.<number>@` or `snco.<number>@`): those stay with their squadron. The master admin account cannot move.

**Only the login moves**: your password, two-step sign-in and name come with you. The old squadron's programmes, history, requirement entries and instructor list stay with it, and you stop seeing them.

## How it goes

1. **Someone asks.** You do, on [Your details](app:/account) under **Move my login to another squadron** (type the number of the squadron you are joining and your password). Or an owner of your squadron asks for you from the Members list in [Squadron settings](squadron-settings); then you get an email and **nothing happens unless you agree**.
2. **Your account is switched off.** From the moment the request is waiting for the other squadron, you are signed out and cannot sign in. Sign-in says so.
3. **An owner of the squadron you join decides.** They are emailed and see the request under **Moves between squadrons** in their Squadron settings. Any one owner can accept, and **they choose your role** (owner, editor or viewer). Or they decline.
4. **It ends.**
   - If accepted, your account is switched on **in the new squadron**, with that role. All your sessions ended, so you sign in again with the same email address and password.
   - If declined, cancelled or not decided within **14 days**, your account is switched back on **in your old squadron**, as before.

You, an owner of your old squadron, or whoever asked can cancel while it waits. The email you get has a link that cancels it even though your account is switched off.

## What is checked

A move is refused when:

- the address is a squadron-claim address, or the account is the master admin's;
- the account is inactive, its email is not confirmed, or it is using a temporary password;
- you are the **only owner** of your squadron (make someone else an owner first, or delete the squadron);
- there is no squadron with that number, it is your own squadron, it has no active owner, or it has an invitation waiting for your address (ask them to revoke it);
- a move for you is already waiting (one at a time).

While a move waits, an owner cannot switch the account on from the Members list.

## Who is told, and the record

You, the owners of both squadrons and, at the end, everyone concerned are emailed at each step. Both squadrons' security logs get a line for every step (asked, agreed, accepted, declined, cancelled, expired).
