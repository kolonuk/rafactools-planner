# Instructors

The programme grid has an **Instructor** box for each classification on each parade
night. To save typing, the browser suggests names as you type. You can always type
any name instead: the suggestions are a convenience, not a restriction.

## What gets suggested

1. **All Staff**.
2. The people in your squadron's **instructor list** (below), written with their rank,
   for example `Fg Off Smith`.
3. Every **member** of your squadron, by the name they have set for themselves (see
   [your name](#your-name)) or, if they have not set one, their email address.

A name that would appear more than once is only suggested once.

## The instructor list

Owners and editors can keep the list. Open **Instructors** in the top bar (or
**Manage instructors** at the top of a programme).

- **Add someone:** enter their **Name** (required, up to 80 characters) and optionally
  their **Rank** (up to 40 characters, for example `Sgt`), then select **Add**.
- **Edit someone:** select **Edit** beside them to change their name or rank (the same limits and
  duplicate rule apply). Names already typed into programmes are free text and are not changed.
- **Remove someone:** select **Remove** beside them. Names already typed into
  programmes are not changed.
- The same name with the same rank cannot be added twice. The same name with a
  different rank is a different entry.
- A list holds up to 200 people.

The list is for staff who do not need a login, so it holds **only a name and a rank**:
no email address or phone number. It is only visible to your squadron's owners and
editors, and only your own squadron's list is ever shown to you.

## Your name

Everyone who is signed in, including viewers, can set the name shown for them. Select
your name or email in the top bar to open **Your details**, type your name (up to 80
characters) and select **Save**. Leave it blank to show your email address instead.

Your name appears in the members table in [squadron settings](squadron-settings) next to
your email address, and as a suggestion in the grid's Instructor boxes.
