# Getting started

Each squadron is set up once, by one person, who becomes its **owner**.
Everyone else joins by invitation (see [roles and invites](roles-and-invites)).

## Claim your squadron

1. Open **Claim it** on the login page, or go to the [claim page](app:/claim).
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Squadron display name** – optional, for example `1234 (Sample Town) Squadron`.
     If you leave it empty the squadron is called `1234 Squadron`.
   - **Email** – must be your squadron's own address, in the form
     `snco.<number>@<domain>` or `oc.<number>@<domain>` (the page shows the
     domain your site accepts). The number in the address becomes the squadron number.
   - **Password** – 12 to 72 characters.
3. Select **Claim squadron**.

A squadron number can be claimed only once. If someone has already claimed it
you will be told so; ask them to invite you instead.

## Confirm your email

You are sent a confirmation link. It works **once** and expires after
**24 hours**. Until you open it you cannot log in. After opening it you are
taken to the login page.

If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder, then ask whoever runs
the site to check that email sending is set up (see
[configuration](operators-configuration)).
